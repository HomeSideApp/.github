# 🏡 HomeSide

### Your home, organized.

**HomeSide is an open-source, self-hosted platform for managing everyday life at home.**

Organize your personal and shared household life with shopping lists, recipes and financial management — while keeping control over what stays private and what you share.

[🌐 Website](https://homeside.cloud) · [💻 GitHub](https://github.com/HomeSideApp)

---

## ✨ What is HomeSide?

HomeSide brings everyday household management into one place.

Home automation platforms are great at managing devices and automations. HomeSide focuses on the other side of home life: the information, organization and everyday activities of the people who live there.

HomeSide understands that **living together does not mean sharing everything**.

It is designed to be:

* 🌱 **Open source**
* 🏠 **Self-hosted**
* 🔐 **Privacy-first**
* 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 **Built around households**
* 🏘️ **Multi-household**
* 🧩 **Modular by design**

---

## 🚀 Available today

### 🏠 Multiple households

Create multiple independent households and invite other people to each one.

A household can represent your permanent home, a second residence, a shared apartment or even a temporary group such as a weekend getaway.

Being a member of a household does not automatically expose a user's private HomeSide data.

---

### 🧩 Modular households

Every household can enable or disable the modules it needs.

This allows each household to have its own configuration depending on how it is being used.

A permanent family home and a temporary holiday household can therefore use completely different sets of features.

---

### 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Household Dashboard

Each household has its own dashboard providing a shared view of its enabled modules and relevant household information.

---

### 🛒 Shopping Lists

Create shopping lists for yourself or share them with a household.

A shopping list can remain completely **private** or become a **collaborative household list**, allowing members to keep track of what needs to be bought together.

This means you do not need to create a household just to use HomeSide for your own shopping lists.

---

### 🍳 Recipes

Build your own personal cookbook and share selected recipes with the households you belong to.

Recipes remain owned by their author.

Sharing a recipe with a household does not transfer ownership or create a duplicate recipe. Household members can use the shared recipe and, when needed, create their own personal copy.

The same recipe can therefore be shared across multiple households while remaining part of the author's personal cookbook.

---

### 💰 Finances

Track both **personal** and **shared household** expenses and income.

HomeSide separates private financial information from household finances, allowing users to decide which financial information belongs to them personally and which should be managed collaboratively.

---

## 🤖 HomeSide Assistant

HomeSide includes an AI assistant designed to work with the different areas of the platform.

The assistant can use HomeSide context to help users interact with their household information while respecting the separation between personal and shared data.

AI capabilities are designed around user control, with actions that modify data remaining subject to user confirmation.

## 🔐 Personal and shared by design

Privacy in HomeSide is not based simply on whether a user belongs to a household.

Resources can belong to a user's personal space or participate in a household context depending on the module and the resource.

```text
User
│
├── Private space
│   ├── Shopping lists
│   ├── Personal cookbook
│   ├── Personal finances
│   └── Personal settings
│
├── Household A
│   ├── Members
│   ├── Dashboard
│   ├── Enabled modules
│   ├── Shared shopping lists
│   ├── Shared recipes
│   └── Shared finances
│
├── Household B
│   └── ...
│
└── Temporary Household
    ├── Invited members
    └── Only information shared within that household
```

This separation means that participating in a household never implies access to everything another member has in HomeSide.

For example, a group organizing a rural getaway can create a temporary household, invite participants and share shopping lists or other household resources without exposing anyone's private lists, recipes, finances or other personal information.

## 🧠 HomeSide philosophy

HomeSide is being built around a few core principles.

### Your data belongs to you

Self-hosting should remain a first-class way of running HomeSide.

### Shared is a choice

Being part of a household should not mean exposing your personal information.

HomeSide distinguishes between personal resources and household resources, allowing users to decide what belongs to them and what should be shared.

### Households are flexible

A household does not necessarily represent one permanent family home.

Users can belong to multiple households representing different homes, groups or temporary situations without mixing their private information between them.

### Privacy by design

Sensitive information should have appropriate access controls and should only be available to the people who are supposed to see it.

### Complement home automation

HomeSide is not intended to replace platforms such as Home Assistant.

Instead, it focuses on household management and aims to complement the automation ecosystem around your home.
